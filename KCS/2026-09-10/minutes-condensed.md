**6:00pm - Call to Order, Opening Prayer, Dinner Served**

Jerry called the meeting to order and opened with prayer. Dinner was served.

He recalled God's faithfulness through the school's history, including the three occasions on which the Board chose to keep the school open.

**Approval of Agenda and Previous Meeting Minutes**

**FCIS Governance Standards Review - Standard 2.4**

* Jerry read Standard 2.4 on the Board's governance role and the Head of School's separate role in leading staff and day-to-day operations.
* **A governance standard will be reviewed at every Board meeting** and recorded in the minutes, as FCIS requires.

**Finance Office Update - Kris Roth (on behalf of Ana Prinz)**

* Kris presented the Flourish investment update and the May and June 2026 reports. The May and June figures are **not final**; corrections are still being made.
* The 2025-2026 fiscal year closed **ahead of budget**, driven largely by the UKnight Fund.
* Georgina has redistributed the monthly budget based on prior years so it reflects when expenses actually occur.
* **Restricted fund accounting** is now set up in QuickBooks for the UKnight Fund and will be applied to the school's other restricted funds. Restricted gifts must be spent according to the purpose stated in the appeal.
* **The Board will review appeal wording before appeals go out**, so that finance and advancement agree in advance on how funds may be used.
* Pledges are recognized when made, under GAAP. The Board agreed the binding nature of a pledge should be made clear, positively, when the gift is requested.
* **Spending against uncollected pledges:** the Board favored spending up to the amount collected, and bringing any request to spend uncollected funds to the Board for approval. **The finance committee will bring a policy recommendation to the Board.**
* **A quarterly report with prior-year comparisons will be added** now that the accounting basis is consistent year over year.
* **Strategic plan funding:** Kris has mapped each strategic plan goal to a timeline and funding source, to show FCIS that the plan is budgeted. She will place the spreadsheet in the shared folder. The Board will decide which plan items go into the 2027-2028 budget before it is built.

**Head of School Update - Terey Torralbas**

* **Admissions:** The statement of faith is now part of the application. Additional language, including on gender, is being added to the family handbook.
* **Sewer project:** Permits are approved. The bids will be revisited against the engineer's selected option, and the school will ask for bids to be honored as long as possible. Alfie will raise cost sharing with the joint stewardship committee.
* **2027-2028 Tuition:** The finance committee proposed a 5% increase, consistent with what was communicated to families at last year's State of the School.
  * Alfie made a motion to increase tuition by **5% for the 2027-2028 school year**. Diane seconded. **M/S/P.**

**Governance Review - Alfie Hernandez**

* Alfie presented the **Governance Review and Committee Charter Recommendations** (July 20, 2026) from the Governance and Board Development Committee. The review found the governing framework fundamentally sound and recommended no significant changes to the Constitution or church bylaws at this time. It recommends formal charters for three Board workstreams: Governance and Board Development; Strategic Planning and Financial Alignment; and Advancement and Development.
* Items identified for follow-up:
  * The church bylaws' dissolution provision, to address continuity for the school.
  * Board composition requirements under Article IV, with options to be presented to the Session. Alfie will bring a recommendation to the next meeting.
  * Board terms (Article IV, Section 2), a typographical correction (Article IV, Section 3(d)), meeting frequency (Article IV, Section 3(i)), and officer roles (Article V).
* Jerry asked each committee to give a brief status update at every Board meeting.
* Alfie made a motion to adopt the Governance Review and Committee Charter Recommendations as a draft and living document, with the changes discussed at this meeting, including the addition of the stewardship liaison committee. Diane seconded. **M/S/P.**

**Joint Committees - Alfie Hernandez**

* **Cost sharing:** The Session approved a **70/30** cost-sharing arrangement for one year.
* **Use of space:** A working group will study the campus and return two or three options for meeting the school's space needs. This is a fact-gathering stage, not a recommendation.
