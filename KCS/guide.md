# KCS Board Minutes Drafting Guide

Standing rules for drafting Kendall Christian School Board of Trustees meeting minutes from transcripts.

## Style

- **No em dashes.** Use periods, commas, parentheses, or restructure the sentence. Existing minutes use a spaced hyphen (` - `) in section headers.
- **Detail level: ~7/10 by default.** Capture who spoke, what was decided, what the action items are, and the numbers. Don't transcribe every back-and-forth. Go to 9-10/10 for: formal votes, budget and tuition decisions, accreditation matters, governance/bylaws changes, personnel decisions, and anything the Chair flags for the record.
- **First names in the body, full names in the attendance block.** "Jerry called the meeting to order," not "Mr. Zeidler called the meeting to order." (The 12/11/2025 sample uses honorifics; the 2026 minutes moved to first names. Follow the 2026 convention.) Exception: guests and non-Board presenters get "Mrs. Jacky Donate" on first mention, first name after.
- **Bold the numbers.** Dollar figures, enrollment counts, percentages, and vote outcomes.
- **Verbatim standards text goes in italics inside a quote**, e.g. the FCIS indicator language Terey reads aloud.
- Financial tables go in as markdown tables, with a bracketed note that they must be pasted into the Google Doc by hand.

## Document Structure

```
**KCS Board Meeting Minutes**
**Thursday, MM/DD/YYYY 6:00pm**
**Location: Media Center**

**Attending:**
* Full Name, Role          <- note "(virtual)" for Zoom attendees

**Absent:**
* Full Name, Role (reason, if known)

**Guest:**
* Full Name, Title (org)

---

**6:00pm - Call to Order, Opening Prayer, Dinner Served**
**Devotion**
...agenda sections in order, each headed by topic and presenter...
**Open Floor**
**Closing Prayer & Adjournment**

---

**Next scheduled meeting:**
* Thursday, Month D, YYYY - 6:00pm

---

Respectfully submitted,
Doug Hathaway
KCS School Board Member Secretary
```

- Time-stamp section headers when the transcript makes the time recoverable (`**6:51pm - Finance Update - Kris Roth**`). Don't invent times. The 04/30 minutes only timestamp the call to order, which is fine.
- Note the adjournment time if it's recoverable.
- **Motions** follow a fixed form: `Diane made a motion to approve the agenda and prior meeting minutes. Kris seconded. **M/S/P.**` (M/S/P = moved, seconded, passed.)

## Workflow

Two artifacts per meeting, both in `KCS/YYYY-MM-DD/`:

1. **`minutes.md`** - the full draft. Everything of substance from the transcripts, including the devotional in detail, personal/pastoral context, and the full arc of each discussion.
2. **`minutes-condensed.md`** - the tightened version that actually goes out. Compresses the devotional to its text and application, collapses narrative into summary bullets, drops color that doesn't serve the record. Keeps every number, decision, motion, and action item.

Draft the full version first, then condense. Don't skip straight to condensed: the full draft is where details get caught, and it's the fallback when someone asks "what exactly did we say about X."

Leave a `**Drafting notes**` block at the bottom of the draft for open questions, then strip it before the minutes are circulated.

## Record, Don't Audit

Minutes report what the Board said and did. They are not a compliance review.

- Write down findings, positions, and decisions **as the Board stated them**. If someone says the
  Board is out of compliance with the Constitution, that goes in, attributed to the discussion.
- **Don't add your own analysis.** No gap-hunting between the governing documents and current
  practice, no flagging of risks nobody raised, no "this belongs in the minutes alongside X."
- **Don't chase implications.** An offhand remark is not a finding. If it matters, someone at the
  table will say so on the record.
- Open questions are fine in `notes.md` when they affect the accuracy of the draft (a garbled
  number, an unclear motion outcome). They are not fine as governance recommendations.

## Confidentiality and Content to Omit

- **Official meeting business only.** The Board eats dinner together and the recording runs through
  all of it. Table talk before the call to order, and social asides during the meeting, stay out:
  birthdays and cake, family news, church events, who ate what, catching up. Even when a real topic
  comes up in that window (facilities, security, costs), it doesn't go in the minutes unless it is
  raised again on the record. If it seems too useful to lose, park it in the meeting's `notes.md`
  under a "Not for minutes" heading rather than drafting it in.
- **No personnel or family news.** Pregnancies, illnesses, leave plans, and similar belong nowhere in
  the minutes, even when announced to the whole table. Coverage arrangements go in only when the
  Board formally acts on them.

- **FCIS Warned Status is confidential.** The school was placed on Warned Status by letter dated 04/25/2026 for up to three years. Board members are under confidentiality agreements; the designation is not published on the website and does not change the public accreditation listing. It belongs in the minutes (it's Board business), but never in anything that leaves the Board, and never in a public-facing summary.
- **Personnel specifics.** Named teachers tied to credentialing gaps, evaluations, or employment decisions get handled at the level of "the Board discussed rationale documentation for two teachers." Names only where the record genuinely requires them.
- **Donor identities and individual gift amounts.** Donor data access is limited to Jacky, Terey, Georgina, and Ana. The Board sees aggregate campaign totals only. Minutes follow the same rule: aggregates, not individual donors.
- **Church/school cost disputes.** Real Board business and belongs in the record, but keep it to positions, numbers, and next steps. Leave out offhand characterizations of church leadership or Session decisions.
- **Liability-sensitive asides.** Conversation drifts. Offhand legal, medical, or financial assertions, and accusations about people or third parties, stay out. Summarize the decision, not the aside.

## Recurring Sections

Most meetings hit some subset of these. Use the same headings so minutes are diffable year over year.

- Call to Order, Opening Prayer, Dinner Served
- Devotion (note the passage and the application to the Board)
- Approval of Agenda and Previous Meeting Minutes
- FCIS Governance Standards Review (a numbered standard per meeting, read and affirmed)
- Finance Office Update (Flourish investment, monthly reports, projections, multiyear financial planning)
- Head of School Update (admissions, facilities, tuition, accreditation)
- Joint Committees
- Key Dates
- Open Floor
- Closing Prayer & Adjournment
