**7:36pm - FCIS Governance Standards Review - Standard 2.5**

Jerry read Standard 2.5:

> *"The board nurtures a positive working relationship with the head of school, provides feedback as needed, and evaluates him or her on an annual basis."*

* Jerry described the Board's working relationship with the Head of School as very positive, and Terey agreed. Terey confirmed she receives feedback as needed.
* **Head of School evaluation:** Alfie has provided a new template. **Board members will receive it within the next week, and last year's evaluation will be completed by the beginning of November.**

**7:38pm - Finance Update - Kris Roth**

* Kris presented the Flourish investment update. Interest income is now part of the budget, so the Board must be careful about relying on it. **Kris suggested researching whether a better rate is available.** Any decision to move the funds will come on recommendation from the Board.
* Ana and Georgina have separated the restricted funds from the operating budget. In doing so, they found the budget was built on a higher enrollment than the school achieved and was not formally revised afterward.
* **The finance committee will re-budget and bring a revised budget to the Board for approval.** Current projections are close to break-even for operations.
* **UKnight Fund money cannot be used to offset the operating budget.** Designated gifts must be spent according to the appeal's stated purposes. The wording of the **February** appeal is being reviewed to allow more flexibility, with Jacky's input.
* Kris proposed a **set budget calendar**, with a budget completed before tuition is set and a formal re-budget once enrollment is known. Terey agreed the school should plan earlier and on a **multi-year** basis.
* Kris will review with Ana whether each school program covers its costs. She also noted the long-term goal of an **endowment** to support the general fund.
* Kris asked that someone else lead the visionary side of the strategic plan. Terey asked that the admin team be included.
* **A short statement will be drafted for the Session** explaining why revised budget figures are not yet available. Terey will include it in her report to the Session.

**8:03pm - Upper School Update - Lisa [surname], Upper School Principal**

* The **sixth grade has 10 students**, eight returning and two new. Lisa is working to keep fifth-grade families from looking elsewhere. She hopes for two sections each of sixth and seventh grade, and realistically expects one of each.
* Her vision is that students leave eighth grade "marketable," built on engagement, relationships, and rigor. Each class carries a three-year service project. The sixth grade chose **Paws 4 You**, an animal welfare organization.
* Lisa and Terey see **communication** as the school's distinguishing strength. Lisa's goal is a waiting list for the upper school.
* The flexible classroom furniture is working well.
* Terey reminded the Board of its role in holding the school accountable to the mission statement.

**8:17pm - Head of School Update - Terey Torralbas**

* **FCIS visit, March 1:** FCIS will return for one full day with three visitors. They will review the change of program for the added grades, and they will meet with the Board on governance. **The majority of the Board should attend.** Terey, Alfie, and Jerry will prepare **Board governance training** early in the calendar year.
* **Fifth grade and modulars:** This week the fifth graders are moving out of the modulars into the upper wing. The modulars are in poor condition and are being tested for mold. **There will not be room for these classes in August, and a plan is needed quickly.** The long-term need is about five more classrooms.
* **Space options discussed:**
  * reconfiguring space with the church to free a classroom;
  * replacing the modulars, where the rule on replacing portables needs research;
  * modular buildings with bathrooms, as a far less costly alternative to the planned building;
  * a second story.
* Alfie advised making better use of existing space first, then building only what is still short. Additional bathrooms are needed. Alfie is waiting on numbers on a further sewer issue.
* The February fundraiser may be directed to the space need rather than a new playground.
* **Covered outdoor court:** Terey raised a covered, open-sided multipurpose court based on Alfie's fire-station model. It could be used for sports and for church and school events. Lisa noted that sports are key to keeping boys in the upper school.
* Alfie noted that FCIS will expect the strategic plan to tie budget, mission, timelines, and goals together. **Terey will put the space and facilities plan on paper and tie it to financial goals.**

**8:45pm - Governance and Joint Stewardship - Alfie Hernandez**

* The governance committee's initial deliverables are complete.
* **Board composition (Constitution Article IV, Section 1):** Alfie circulated a proposed limited amendment with three options and recommended **Option 1**. Option 1 keeps the requirement that **at least 60%** of Board members belong to Christchurch Miami. It opens the remaining seats to qualified members of other PCA churches or evangelical, Bible-believing churches, consistent with Article II. All three options would go to the Session, with Option 1 as the recommendation.
* Alfie made a motion to adopt Option 1 and forward it to the Session. [Seconder and outcome.]
* **Corporate bylaws:** Alfie has contacted legal counsel about updating the dissolution provision to include KCS. **The target is the January meeting.**
* **Joint stewardship committee:** The committee held its fourth meeting on October 8. Work is underway on an objective cost-sharing methodology and on campus space and utilization. Recommendations will go to the Session. The governance framework is drafted.
