### Annual Budget Planning and Approval Cycle (Kris, email 11/12 6:22pm)
*Source: `attachments/Kris - Annual budget cycle - 2026-11-12.eml`. Use when Kris presents it;
record as presented / adopted per transcript.*
1. **Late July / early August:** finalize the current-year budget and set the preliminary budget
   for the following fiscal year to support tuition planning.
2. **By October 1:** release tuition rates based on the preliminary planning assumptions.
3. **February / March:** review the upcoming year's preliminary budget with updated enrollment
   expectations, focusing on teacher compensation and staffing.

   and shared-cost changes affecting future budget cycles. *Example: shared capital projects given
   to the school by 5/31/2027 go into the FY 2028-2029 budget and cannot commence or expect a
   shared contribution until 6/1/2028.*

   set the next preliminary planning budget.
