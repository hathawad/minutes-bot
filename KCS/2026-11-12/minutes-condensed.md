**6:00pm - Call to Order, Opening Prayer, Dinner Served**

Alfie called the meeting to order and opened with prayer. Dinner was served.

Alfie shared a devotion from **Psalm 119:97-104**: true wisdom comes from God and is shown by walking in obedience to His word, not simply knowing it. The Board has been entrusted with a ministry to children and families, and its greatest asset is a collective willingness to humble itself and seek God's will as faithful stewards. Alfie thanked Jerry for more than 30 years of service, and Diane and Jim for their faithful service, as he began his first meeting as Chair.

**Approval of Agenda**

* **The agenda was approved.** The previous meeting minutes are still being amended and will be distributed when complete.

**FCIS Governance Standards Review - Standard 2.6**

* Alfie read Standard 2.6 on board development, recruitment and orientation, continuing education, and Board self-evaluation. These match the Governance committee's deliverables.
* Alfie and Terey are arranging **continuing education** for the Board.

**New Board Member**

* Jerry made a motion to appoint **Tom Gonzalez** to the Board of Trustees. Diane seconded. **M/S/P.** Tom will serve on the finance committee.

**Constitution**

* The Board's recommendation on **Board composition (Article IV, Section 1)** was amended and **approved by the Session on October 27, 2026**: at least 60 percent of trustees shall be members of Christchurch Miami, and the remaining members may be communing members in good standing of PCA or other biblically faithful churches who subscribe to Article II of the school constitution.
* Alfie read **Article II** (Basis, Purpose, and Principles), the one article that cannot be amended, and noted the strategic plan is aligned with it.

**Finance Office Update - Kris Roth (on behalf of Ana Prinz)**

* Kris presented the Flourish investment update. The Board's question on Flourish rates versus CDs was answered; holdings are monitored and will be adjusted as CDs mature.
* Kris asked that financial materials be finalized and shared ahead of the meeting rather than revised on the day. Alfie asked that agendas and materials go out earlier.
* Diane made a motion to approve the **revised 2026-2027 budget**. Jim seconded. **M/S/P.**
* The **preliminary 2027-2028 planning budget was tabled.** The question of reserves goes to the next finance committee meeting.
* Jim made a motion to adopt the **annual budget planning and approval calendar** proposed by Kris. Diane seconded. **M/S/P.** The calendar:
  * Late July / early August: finalize the current-year budget and set the next year's preliminary budget.
  * By October 1: release tuition rates.
  * February / March: review the preliminary budget, focusing on compensation and staffing.
  * By May 31: obtain the church's capital project and shared-cost information for the following budget cycle.
* Alfie reported the church will move to the same accounting platform as the school on January 1, which should help align budget cycles.

**In Memory of Colleen Zanardi - Jerry Zeidler**

* Jerry remembered Colleen Zanardi, the school's Event Coordinator, who recently passed away. She served the school as an employee, a parent, and a church member.
* Doug made a motion to continue the family's employee tuition discount for the children through their time at KCS. Kris seconded. **Passed unanimously.** The Board will follow up with a policy for future situations.
* Kris made a motion to have **Jacky Donate explore a scholarship fund in Colleen's name**. Diane seconded. **M/S/P.**

**Facilities - Alfie Hernandez**

* **Sewer project:** Permits are approved and bids came in higher than expected. Alfie will ask the county whether the existing septic system can be kept or expanded before the Board commits. Cost sharing with the church will be discussed.
* **Modulars:** The two existing modulars can no longer be used as classrooms, and students have been moved into the main buildings. Two new modulars are the likely plan for **August 2027**. The joint committee on space meets next week and will report to the Board.

**Work Streams Update**

* **Governance and Board Development:** The Constitution review and the charters for all three work streams are complete. The governance manual, orientation and recruitment frameworks, Board calendar, and self-evaluation process are in progress.
* **Strategic Planning and Financial Alignment:** The budget calendar adopted at this meeting completes a deliverable.
* **Advancement and Development:** Tom, Jacky, and Terey will hold a first meeting. School merchandise is selling well, with profits going to the UKnight Fund. **The Board is awaiting Tom's guidance on sales tax for merchandise.**
* Alfie asked each member to keep a copy of the work stream charters and deliverables as part of the Board manual.

**Governance**

* The Board will meet **every four weeks**, in keeping with the Constitution's requirement to meet at least monthly.
* Alfie will consult an attorney about the school's incorporation and the dissolution provision (Article IX) of the church's corporate bylaws, ahead of the church's corporate meeting in January.

**Head of School Update - Terey Torralbas**

* Terey updated the Board on the pending lawsuit, which is awaiting a trial date.

**Key Dates**

* FCIS visit: **March 1, 2027**
* Next Board meeting: **Thursday, December 10, 2026**

**Open Floor**

* **Wayside:** Cornerstone Church is taking over Wayside and has worked well with KCS. They intend to strengthen the arrangement for the long term.
* **Upper school enrollment:** Lisa reported on seventh-grade re-enrollment. Most families intend to stay, and she asked the Board to help spread the word about the middle school program.
* **Sports:** Alfie will price a covered, lighted outdoor basketball court and bring a proposal ahead of the February UKnight Fund campaign.
* Copies of the NAIS Trustee's Handbook will be ordered for any member who needs one.

**Closing Prayer & Adjournment**

* Diane made a motion to adjourn. Kris seconded. **M/S/P.**
* Jerry led the closing prayer. The meeting adjourned at 7:55pm.
