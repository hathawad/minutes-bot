**6:00pm - Call to Order, Opening Prayer, Dinner Served**

Alfie called the meeting to order and opened with prayer. Dinner was served.

**Devotion**

Alfie shared a devotion from **Psalm 119:97-104**, drawing on a devotional by Tim Keller. He focused on verse 100, "I have more understanding than the elders, for I obey your precepts." The psalmist is not dismissing experience, education, or counsel; he is saying that true wisdom comes from God and is shown through obedience to His word. Wisdom is not simply knowing the right path but choosing to walk it, and it is received through humility, obedience, love, and delight in God's word.

Alfie applied this to the Board: it has been entrusted with something greater than budgets, buildings, policies, and strategic plans, a ministry that touches the lives of children and families. Sound governance, financial stewardship, and long-term sustainability matter, but the purpose is to honor Christ. There will be difficult decisions, disagreements, and times when the way forward is unclear. The Board's greatest asset is not individual knowledge or experience but a collective willingness to humble itself before the Lord and seek His will. "We're not here to build personal legacies. We're here to be faithful stewards of what God has placed in our hands."

Alfie expressed his personal appreciation to **Jerry** for more than **30 years** of faithful service, and to **Diane** and **Jim** for their faithful service and contributions. The Board is building on a foundation they helped establish. As he assumes the role of Chair, Alfie said his prayer is that the Board will honor those efforts by continuing to lead with humility, integrity, and a desire to serve Christ. This was his first meeting as moderator.

**Constitution**

* **Board composition (Article IV, Section 1):** The Board's recommendation (Option 1), approved at the October meeting, went to the Session, which amended and **approved it on October 27, 2026**. The adopted text:

  > *"At least 60 percent of the members of the Board of Trustees shall be members of Christchurch Miami. The remaining members may be communing members in good standing of PCA churches or other biblically faithful churches, provided they subscribe to Article II of the school constitution."*

  The Session removed a closing clause referring to "the established limitation." In response to a question, Alfie clarified that it is the individual member, not the member's church, who must affirm Article II.
* **Article II - Basis, Purpose, and Principles:** Alfie read Article II aloud and noted it is the only article of the Constitution that cannot be amended.
  * *Basis:* the infallible Word of God as interpreted by the doctrinal standards of Kendall Presbyterian Church (now Christchurch Miami), the Westminster Confession of Faith and its catechisms.
  * *Purpose:* to establish and maintain a school providing quality Christian education that prepares students as God-fearing, worthy members of society, church, and state.
  * *Principles:* that everything has been created so that the Triune God may be glorified in and through it; that the home, with the school and church in supporting roles, is responsible for a God-honoring education of children; and that only through a saving knowledge of Jesus Christ can anyone have eternal life.
* Alfie reviewed the strategic plan against Article II and found it on point with the school's mission.

*Annual Budget Planning and Approval Calendar*

* Kris presented a budget calendar developed by the finance committee so budget revisions are not missed again. It will be put on a calendar with reminders:
  1. **Late July / early August:** finalize the current-year budget using enrollment, and set the preliminary budget for the following year to support tuition planning.
  2. **By October 1:** release tuition rates based on the preliminary planning assumptions.
  3. **February / March:** review the upcoming year's preliminary budget with updated enrollment expectations, with particular attention to teacher compensation and staffing.
  4. **By May 31:** obtain preliminary information from the church on significant capital projects and shared-cost changes. For example, shared capital projects given to the school by 5/31/2027 will be included in the FY 2028-2029 budget and cannot begin or expect a shared contribution until 6/1/2028.
  5. **Late July / early August:** finalize the upcoming year's budget and set the next preliminary planning budget.
* **Church alignment:** The church has noted that its **40-year inspection** is approaching, with projects needed in **2028**. Alfie reported the church will move to the same accounting platform as the school (QuickBooks) on **January 1**, which should help align the budget cycles, and he will make sure information flows both ways. Jim noted the church reports to its members in January on a calendar-year basis; Kris noted that does not prevent a fiscal-year budget.
* Jim made a motion to adopt the annual budget calendar as proposed by Kris. Diane seconded. **M/S/P.**
* Kris noted the budget calendar completes a **Strategic Planning work stream deliverable**.

**Facilities - Alfie Hernandez**

*Sewer Project*

* Engineering plans and permits for connecting to the county sewer are approved. Bids are coming in at upwards of **$500,000**, which is still below the earlier pump-station estimate of more than **$600,000**.
* Before committing, Alfie will ask the county whether the existing septic system (rated for about **400** students) can be kept or enlarged, or whether proximity to the sewer line requires a hookup. *Two Existing Modulars (Two Classrooms by August 2027)*

* The joint committee on space is still working on whether church administrative offices will move, which could free about two classrooms. Alfie had priced two new state- and county-approved, hurricane-rated modulars.
* **The two existing modulars can no longer be used as classrooms.** Students have been consolidated into the main buildings, and the old units may remain as storage. This sets the plan back two classrooms.
* The likely need is two 24x50 or 24x60 modulars (capacity about **80** students), which are permitted by code, with a lead time of two to three months. Location is still open; easements rule out the area behind the current units.
* Terey and Alice Hernandez are mapping church and school space use for the joint committee, which meets next week, and will report to the Board. Classrooms are needed by **August 2027** for fifth- and seventh-grade growth, so decisions must come soon.
* Alfie has preliminary drawings for a permanent classroom building but called it premature. **The immediate priority is determining what is needed for fall and when to commit.** Alfie plans to walk the property with Pastor James Drake.
