Dear Teresa,

On behalf of the FCIS Board of Directors, we extend our appreciation for your engagement in the recent accreditation evaluation process. We recognize the time, effort, and openness demonstrated by your community in preparing for and participating in the visit. Following careful review of the Evaluation Team's report, the Commission on Accreditation and the FCIS Board of Directors have voted to place Kendall Christian School on **Warned Status** for a period of up to three years. This designation indicates that, while the school continues to demonstrate important strengths, there are areas requiring focused attention to ensure full alignment with FCIS Standards. In order to move from Warned Status to Continued Accreditation, the school must address the following:

- Ensure that the Head of School, Board Chair, and incoming Board Chair attend the annual Leadership Partners through the Principles of Good Governance workshop (Standard 2.6).
- Consider securing a subscription to resources such as ISM or BoardSource to strengthen the Board's understanding of its roles and responsibilities (Standards 2.6 and 2.8).
- Review the Strategic Plan and ensure that the multi-year financial plan fully supports the strategic initiatives outlined therein (Standard 2.8).
- Consider establishing a Development Committee of the Board to provide strategic support for advancement efforts and the Strategic Plan (Standard 2.8).
- Regularly review and update the bylaws to ensure alignment with current school practices (Standard 2.2).
- Develop and implement a Conflict of Interest policy (Standard 2.3).
- Clarify and update the succession plan for the Head of School and Board leadership (Standard 2.10).
- Provide documentation supporting the rationale for employing teachers who do not meet established standards (Standards 7.3, 7.4, and 10.3).

We are confident that, with sustained attention and commitment, Kendall Christian School can successfully address these areas and strengthen its alignment with FCIS Standards.
