**Cost Analysis - Shared Expenses**

* **Context:** Roughly a year since the church relaunch. The church is consuming more shared cost, but allocation percentages have not changed since before the relaunch.

* **Industry benchmarks (Kris's research):** In healthy church/school relationships where the school is a ministry, schools typically pay **10-20%** of budget on shared costs. KCS pays ~9% in total but **also funds its own admin and janitorial**. Across categories the split is roughly **82% school / 18% church** — heavy on the school. Standard allocation methods: square footage, headcount, usage hours, or actual usage. KCS is paying 80-90% of shared facility/admin categories.

* **Per-student comparison (Anna):**
  * Kendall (KPC): **$2,370.97/student**
  * Wayside: **$1,640/student**
  * KCS pays ~40%+ more per student at Kendall, despite not being a ministry of the church at Wayside. The Wayside number includes elevated current-year renovations expected to decline.

* **Cost pressures:** Electric bill up ~$1,000/month (consumes half the projected $30K surplus). Wear-and-tear on school-owned furniture and electronics used by the church (e.g., Sunday's smart-board failure). Grounds maintenance (~$23K) and lawn/landscaping currently 100% school. Building maintenance ~$35.9K, campus enhancement ~$15K, equipment ~$30K need allocation review.

* **Alfie's reminder:** Two budgets were approved (operating + revitalization-designated). Revitalization-driven costs should draw from the designated fund, not operating shared-cost lines. The Board needs a comparison report showing **what costs have increased since the revitalization began**.

* **Accounting mismatch (Kris):** School is accrual; church is cash. A $400K roof appears as a single-year loss on church books vs. ~30-year depreciation on school books. This will become an obstacle for middle-school financing or any consolidated banking application. **Tom Gonzales** (CPA, in deacon training) may help drive a church transition to accrual.

* **Working relationship update:** **Alice** (office/property manager) is now meeting with Terey weekly, serving as the conduit between school and church under Session authority. Visibility into building activity has improved meaningfully.

* **Recent adjustments:** Last three church/school finance meetings have been cordial and produced minor adjustments, including 50/50 splits on the **roof** and the **pool air conditioner**. By December the school was already $8-9K over budget on utilities, projecting ~$30K of unbudgeted utility spend over the year — alone consuming the budgeted surplus.

  6. **Develop and implement a Conflict of Interest policy (Std 2.3).**
  7. **Clarify and update succession plan for Head and Board leadership (Std 2.10).**
