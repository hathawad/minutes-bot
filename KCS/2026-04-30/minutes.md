**6:00pm - Call to Order, Opening Prayer, Dinner Served**

Jerry called the meeting to order and opened by reflecting on **legacy** at KCS. He noted that he has served on the Board under every Head of School since the school's founding, beginning with Mrs. Arlette Perry, who led the school from its preschool beginnings.

Jerry shared the passing of **John Moore**, who died this week after an eight-month battle with brain cancer. The Moore family has been deeply woven into the life of KPC/Christ Church and KCS:

* **Jerry Moore** (John's father) was a longtime elder at the church and one of the elders who voted to begin the school. Jerry Moore later attended Christ Fellowship and passed away a few years ago.
* All three of Jerry Moore's sons attended KCS, and their children attended as well.
* John was married to **Kelly Moore**, the surviving flight attendant from the 1982 Air Florida Flight 90 crash into the Potomac. Kelly has shared her testimony at women's events at the church through the years.
* John and Kelly's three sons all went through KCS. Their youngest, **Gregory**, was severely injured in a highway accident roughly a decade ago and passed away after about a year and a half.
* John's memorial service is being held at Christ Fellowship.

Jerry led the Board in opening prayer, thanking God for the legacy of faithfulness over the school's **53 years**, for the elders who had the vision to begin the school, and for the upcoming launch of middle school. He asked God to give the Board His mind for the business of the meeting and to help the Board move in step with His leading. The prayer was followed by a **moment of silence** for the Moore family and for others Board members wished to lift up.

Dinner was served.
