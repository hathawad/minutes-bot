# OCPC Diaconate Minutes Drafting Guide

Standing rules for drafting OCPC Diaconate meeting minutes from transcripts.

## Style

- **No em dashes.** Use periods, commas, parentheses, or restructure the sentence.
- **Detail level: ~7/10 by default.** Summarize discussion crisply: who spoke, what was decided, what the action items are. Don't transcribe every back-and-forth. Crank detail up to 9–10/10 only for important topics: major decisions, formal votes, policy changes, large dollar commitments, safety/liability matters, or topics where the chair has explicitly asked for thorough documentation.

## Content to Omit

- **Firearms / 9:21 team:** Exclude discussion of firearms or 9:21 team operations (tactics, armed coverage, weapons) from the minutes. Drop these segments entirely — do not summarize them. Acceptable to mention 9:21 only in benign operational logistics (e.g., DoD acting as liaison with the 9:21 team for a given Sunday) or as a brief name-origin footnote (1 Chronicles 9:21 — the Levitical gatekeepers of the tabernacle). Anything beyond that — what they discuss, train on, or carry — stays out.

- **Liability-sensitive specifics:** Avoid recording specifics that could create liability exposure for the church. The meetings themselves are formal, but conversation often drifts and attendees mention things in passing that may not be accurate or may not reflect the official stance of the church. Default to summarizing decisions and action items rather than transcribing offhand claims, opinions, legal/medical/financial assertions, or accusations about people or third parties. If in doubt, leave it out or generalize.
