## For Minutes — "9:21 Team" Name Origin

If the 9:21 team is referenced in the minutes (operationally, e.g., DoD acting as liaison), include a brief footnote on the namesake:

> The 9:21 team takes its name from **1 Chronicles 9:21** (ESV) — "Zechariah the son of Meshelemiah was gatekeeper at the entrance of the tent of meeting." The surrounding passage (1 Chr. 9:17–27) describes the Levitical gatekeepers appointed by David and Samuel to keep watch over the gates of the house of the LORD.
